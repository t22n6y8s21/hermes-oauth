Hermes — personal automation

Home · Privacy Policy · Terms of Service

Hermes is a private, single-user desktop automation tool. It is not distributed, sold, or offered to anyone other than its owner.

It uses Google APIs (Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets, and Contacts) only to read and organize the owner's own data on the owner's own computer — for example, showing today's calendar events and reading the owner's own mail.

Hermes は、所有者本人だけが使う個人用のデスクトップ自動化ツールです。配布・販売はしておらず、本人以外は利用しません。Google API は、本人の PC 上で本人のデータを読み、整理するためだけに使います。
