Privacy Policy

Home · Privacy Policy · Terms of Service

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Hermes is used only by its owner. The only Google account that authorizes it is the owner's own account.

  • Data accessed: the owner's Gmail messages, calendar events, Drive files, Docs, Sheets, and contacts, as permitted by the scopes the owner grants.
  • Use: the data is processed on the owner's own computer to automate the owner's personal tasks (such as daily schedule summaries).
  • Sharing: the data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with third parties.
  • Storage: OAuth tokens are stored on the owner's computer and in the owner's private password manager.
  • Revocation: access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Hermes's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

本アプリは所有者本人のみが使用し、本人の Google データを本人の PC 上で個人的な作業の自動化にだけ使います。第三者への提供・販売・広告利用はしません。アクセスは Google アカウントの設定からいつでも取り消せます。
